Day view — gesture-first calendar

The most important screen of the React Native migration. Rebuilds frontend/src/components/calendar/day-view.tsx for touch: long-press-to-create, pan-to-move with a 15-min snap grid, a thumb-reachable FAB and bottom tab nav. 64 dp / hour grid (per docs/react-native-migration.md). Work hours 9:00–17:00 are the work zone; before/after is tinted non-work. Task states mirror frontend/src/lib/task-card.ts (fluid · fixed · overdue · conflict · completed).
