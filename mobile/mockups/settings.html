Pickers — bottom sheets

Tapping any row with a chevron (Start/End, Timezone, Duration adjustments, Profile) slides up a @gorhom/bottom-sheet over the dimmed screen — same overlay/sheet chrome as task-sheets.html's create/edit sheets, not the OS-native picker. The selected option gets a tinted border + checkmark; the duration-mode sheet ports the three cards straight from duration-mode-field.tsx (icon, name, blurb). The profile sheet's Email field is locked — there's no PATCH /users/me for it yet, and email doubles as the OTP sign-in identity — only Name is editable.
