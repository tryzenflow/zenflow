Settings — one flat scroll

Mirrors mobile/app/(app)/settings.tsx exactly: a single scrollable screen, section-labelled like iOS Settings, not a home list + per-section detail screens (that two-level nav was a dead end — a phone has plenty of room to scroll three short cards, and splitting them cost an extra tap + back-chevron for no benefit). Section order: Profile · Appearance · Account. Working hours and Work days were dropped along with User.workStart/workEnd/workDays in the education-pivot migration (20260823155537_add_education_pivot) — the scheduler no longer constrains placement to a configured working window. Timezone is gone from this screen too: it's captured once, automatically, at OTP signup (an x-timezone request header) and is no longer user-editable, so there's nothing left here to show or change — the onboarding wizard that used to confirm it is gone as well (see index.html). There's no "Insights" section (the preference heatmap was removed along with components/settings/insights-panel.tsx — not worth the surface area right now) and no separate "Scheduling" section either (it only ever existed to host the now-deleted duration-adjustment-mode picker). Account gains a "Connect your DLU account" row ahead of Sign out, for the DLU LMS/portal credential hand-off — see dlu-connect.html for that flow's own states.
