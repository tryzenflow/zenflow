Connect your DLU account

Reached from Settings → Account. Two things Zenflow can watch — the LMS and the student portal — each connected on its own. The screen is a short list, not a form: a row per service, and tapping Connect opens a small sign-in sheet. A saved password is never shown again. Dev notes: docs/thesis-scope.md §3.2, issue #26; LMS and portal are separate logins, so each is its own credential pair with its own connect / disconnect lifecycle.
