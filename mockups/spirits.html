Linh thú

Blips are renamed Linh thú (en: Spirits). Side view, flat fills, smooth curves, no outlines. Each one carries the flow mark, the logo curve, on its flank. Every shape takes its colour from a role variable, so one drawing renders in colour or as a locked silhouette. Replaces landing/src/components/Blip.astro and reshapes #259.
