Day view — gesture-first calendar

The most important screen of the React Native migration. Rebuilds frontend/src/components/calendar/day-view.tsx for touch: long-press-to-create, pan-to-move with a 15-min snap grid, a thumb-reachable FAB and bottom tab nav. 64 dp / hour grid (per docs/react-native-migration.md). A scrollable full 12 AM–11:59 PM timeline, mirroring frontend/src/components/calendar/day-column-background.tsx's always-24-hour grid — the work-hours shading (zone-nonwork) is gone along with User.workStart/workEnd/workDays in the education-pivot migration (20260823155537_add_education_pivot); every hour renders the same way now. Session states mirror packages/core/src/task-card.ts — one colour per type (fluid task · assignment · exam · lecture · DND) plus a client-side conflict overlay; there is no overdue or completed state. Tasks under 30 minutes render as a single compact row (title left, time right via justify-between) instead of the stacked two-line layout, mirroring scheduled-block-item.tsx's isCompact branch. Timetable lectures and exams (DLU thesis-scope §3.2/§4) skip the Inbox/Confirm flow entirely — the watcher places them straight onto the calendar and just notifies the student, since there's no ambiguity to resolve the way there is for a scraped assignment. See the "Timetable & exams" section below for how that's surfaced (one consolidated notification per import, not one per lecture) and the long-press "study session before this" shortcut on exam blocks.

Timetable & exams

Unlike assignments, a detected timetable or exam is placed directly onto the calendar and the student is just notified — no Inbox tap-to-confirm needed, since a class time or exam slot from the official portal isn't something to second-guess the way a scraped assignment description is. Auto-imported blocks carry a small institution badge so they read as distinct from manually created tasks. Importing a semester's timetable would mean dozens of individual lecture occurrences, so lectures and exams each get their own single summary toast (one per kind, not one per class) rather than a blended count — and each toast matches the plain bg-popover + colored-icon style used everywhere else in this file, not a fully-tinted card.

Connection needs attention

If the student changes their LMS or portal password outside Zenflow, the next background check fails auth and the watcher goes blind for that provider — this can't wait for a quiet Inbox item, so it surfaces as an urgent, persistent toast instead: destructive-tinted icon + border, tap-to-act like the LMS tap-to-act toast, and it stays until dismissed or fixed (not on the normal auto-dismiss timer). Tapping it deep-links straight to that provider's card in dlu-connect.html.
