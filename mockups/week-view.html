Week View — swipeable day pages

The RN redesign of frontend/src/components/calendar/week-view.tsx + week-grid.tsx. The 7-column desktop grid becomes a horizontal FlatList of full-height single-day timelines (snapToInterval = screenWidth), under a sticky 7-chip week header. Adjacent days "peek" at the screen edge to advertise the swipe. Each day's timeline is the full 24-hour scroll range (12 AM–11:59 PM, matching day-column-background.tsx's always-24-hour grid), with non-work hours tinted zone-nonwork grey — frames are shown scrolled to the populated work-hours portion. Today = Tue Jun 30, 2026; week of Jun 29 – Jul 5.
