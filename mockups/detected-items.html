Detected-item inbox & push

Mobile-only (docs/thesis-scope.md §4, issue #34) — closes the automatic path: a watcher notices something new on a DLU student's LMS/portal → it's written straight onto the calendar as a real session → the student gets a notification that deep-links to that session. There is no confirm step: the item already exists, so tapping a notification just navigates to the week view on that day and plays the same scale + ring "landing" flash the calendar uses right after an edit. A small bell affordance on Day/Week/Month's app bar (not pictured per-screen here — same top-bar chrome as day-view.html, just with a badge) opens the Inbox; in-app, the badge and the list update live over an SSE stream rather than by polling GET /notifications. A push that arrives while the app is foregrounded shows a tap-to-act toast over whatever screen is open; tapping it does the same jump-to-session.

Inbox

Reached from the bell affordance — everything the watchers have flagged that you haven't opened or dismissed, unread first. Rows share the web notification-bell.tsx chrome exactly: the calendar type's own icon and tint (assignment teal, exam rose, lecture sky). Each row carries a kind badge — New / Change / Drop — then a spelled-out relative time (12 minutes ago, not 12m) and, for an assignment/exam/lecture, its fixed due / clock time; the redundant “Portal” / count / “View session” text is gone. Unread rows get a red dot and a bold meta line; a New item (a fresh timetable, exam or assignment) also shows a red ! on the right for importance. The materializer groups a whole term's lectures into one “Timetable for semester 1 is available” row and handles upstream deletions (a cancelled class / withdrawn exam shows as a Drop). The dismiss hint sits at the top so it stays put as the list grows; swiping a row left reveals Dismiss (DELETE /notifications/:id), which on web is the hover ✕ instead.
