Month View — paginated grid + overflow sheet

The RN redesign of frontend/src/components/calendar/month-view.tsx + month-grid.tsx + month-cell.tsx. The desktop popover for "+N more" is replaced by a bottom sheet; months paginate via a horizontal FlatList; tapping a day routes to its day view; long-press drags a task between cells. Today = Tue Jun 30, 2026.
