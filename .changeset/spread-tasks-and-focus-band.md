Spread a task out as a daily amount until its deadline, and split a total into sittings of nearly equal length. A series can now take up to three sittings a day, so a near deadline no longer reads as not enough time. Onboarding preselects morning, shows an icon per time of day, and places sleep and meals around the hours you focus best. The empty day slot sits at that time too. Search ignores accents, the streak counts only tasks, and marks and timetable notices open without errors.
