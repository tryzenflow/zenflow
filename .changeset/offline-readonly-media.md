Offline read-only mode: the calendar cache is warmed for this week and the surrounding months, task details open offline, create/edit/delete are hidden, integrations are greyed out with a red failed-sync style, and there is a dev "Toggle offline" menu item. Notes get a themed audio/video player, a zoomable image/video viewer and offline media placeholders. Link previews load by default, link and audio cards are no longer cut off at the bottom, and the audio speed chip keeps a fixed width so the waveform doesn't shift. In the editor an audio upload is a link like any document and video plays with the default controls; the themed player is view-only. Uploads are capped at 20 MB per file and 100 MB per user, and the too-large toast shows the right size.
